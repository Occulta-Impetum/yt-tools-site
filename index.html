YT-Tools

YT-Tools is a private, personal project used to collect, preserve, and analyze data from the owner's own YouTube channel and to support related YouTube automation.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What YT-Tools does

YT-Tools uses authorized Google and YouTube APIs to retrieve channel, video, analytics, caption, and project file data needed for the owner's own workflows.

Who uses it

YT-Tools is not a public consumer service. It is operated for the owner's personal use and is not offered as a hosted service to other users.

How data is handled

Data is used only to provide the project's requested functionality. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for unrelated purposes.

Google API access

YT-Tools may request access to YouTube channel information, YouTube Analytics, captions, and Google Drive files created or managed by the application. Access is limited to what is needed for the project and is authorized by the account owner.

For details on collection, storage, retention, sharing, and deletion, see the Privacy Policy.
